How it works

From "this thing is driving me mad" to fixed.

Greylock Trust is a marketplace of vetted technology advisors. You tell us what you need and how you like to work. We score every advisor against that and put the best fits first. You book, you meet, it gets sorted.

The whole journey

Five steps, and only two need you

01
About two minutes

You answer the questionnaire

Most directories ask for a zip code and stop. We ask what is actually broken, how soon you need it, whether you want to understand the fix or just have it work, which personality you would rather sit beside, and what you might need years from now.

  • What you need help with — six specialties, choose as many as apply
  • Urgency, from "broken right now" through to "planning ahead"
  • Why you want an advisor: ongoing support, trust, security, purchases, learning, business, family
  • How you like to learn — watch, follow along, read after, ask as you go, or just have it handled
  • Preferred personality: patient teacher, friendly conversationalist, efficient problem solver, highly technical expert, calm and reassuring
  • Working style — quick replies, evenings, weekends, advance scheduling, recurring check-ins
  • Meeting preference and a comfortable hourly budget

Any question can be skipped, and preferences only reorder your matches — they never remove anyone from the list.

02
Instant

We score every advisor against your answers

Each advisor gets a match percentage, drawn as a ring on their card. It is not a popularity score and it is not paid placement — it is your answers weighed against their specialties, rating, availability, rate, and working style. Two different people see two different orders.

03
As long as you like

You compare real profiles

Every profile carries an hourly rate, a single overall rating with the full review breakdown, specialties, personality, next availability, a written biography, and a Q&A in that advisor's own words. Filter by specialty, rate, personality, availability, or meeting type at any point.

04
Under a minute

You book straight into their calendar

No phone tag, no waiting for a callback. Their real availability appears in a month grid; pick a day, pick a time, confirm. Most advisors offer a free 20-minute intro call, which is the lowest-risk way to begin.

  • Free intro call — 20 minutes, no charge, no obligation to book further
  • Full session — typically one to two hours, billed to the minute after the first hour
  • Reschedule or cancel from your confirmation email
05
Ongoing

It stays fixed

Follow-up on anything you worked on together is included. Advisors keep private notes on your devices and setup, so the second visit never starts from zero — and some offer recurring check-ins that catch problems before they become the reason you call.
