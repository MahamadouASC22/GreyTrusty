Your public profile

What clients see, controlled by you

Headshot, title, years of experience, specialties, personality, hourly rate, and the areas you can help clients plan for. Your answers to the same questionnaire clients take are what drive your match scores.

  • ◆  Set your own hourly rate, change it any time

Clients

Notes that make you look brilliant

A private record per client: devices, what you changed, what to check next visit. Clients love being remembered, and it is the difference between a one-off job and a decade-long relationship.

Payouts

Paid weekly, no invoicing

Clients are charged through the platform after the session. Greylock takes a flat platform fee; the rest lands in your account weekly with a plain statement of every visit.
